# Analytics Q&A Copilot — Business Glossary

## Database Tables & Column Names (Actual Schema)

### customers
- customer_id, name, email, country, signup_date
- Example: customer_id=1, name="Alice Smith", country="India"

### products
- product_id, name, category, price, stock
- category is a text column: "Electronics", "Clothing", "Home", "Sports", "Books"
- stock = units remaining in inventory

### orders
- order_id, customer_id, order_date, total_amount, status
- status values: "delivered", "shipped", "pending", "cancelled", "returned"
- order_date format: YYYY-MM-DD (use strftime in SQLite)

### order_items
- item_id, order_id, product_id, quantity, price
- Links orders to products (line items)

### sales_targets
- target_id, year, month, target_amount, category
- year = 4-digit year (2024, 2025)
- month = integer 1-12
- category matches products.category

## Key Business Definitions

### Customer Segments
- VIP Customer: total order amount across all delivered orders > 5000
- Regular Customer: total order amount between 1000 and 5000
- New Customer: total order amount below 1000

### Inventory Rules
- Low Stock: stock < 50 units in products table
- Out of Stock: stock = 0

### Order Status
- delivered: completed, counts toward revenue
- shipped: in transit, counts toward revenue
- pending: not yet processed
- cancelled: voided, exclude from revenue
- returned: goods sent back, exclude from revenue

### Revenue Calculations
- Gross Revenue: SUM(total_amount) WHERE status IN ('delivered','shipped')
- Net Revenue: Gross Revenue excluding returned orders
- Sales Attainment: (Actual Sales / target_amount) * 100

## Date Conventions (SQLite)
- All dates stored as TEXT in YYYY-MM-DD format
- Monthly filter: strftime('%Y-%m', order_date) = '2025-03'
- Year filter: strftime('%Y', order_date) = '2025'
- Month number filter: strftime('%m', order_date) = '03'
- For sales_targets use: WHERE year=2025 AND month=3
