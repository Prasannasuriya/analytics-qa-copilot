# Analytics Q&A Copilot — Business Glossary

## Customer Segments
- **VIP Customer**: Any customer whose total order value exceeds $10,000.
- **Regular Customer**: Total order value between $1,000 and $10,000.
- **New Customer**: Total order value below $1,000 or account age < 90 days.

## Inventory Terms
- **Low Stock**: Fewer than 50 units remaining in the inventory table.
- **Out of Stock**: 0 units remaining — must be excluded from active sales reports.
- **Overstock**: More than 500 units for a single SKU.

## Order Status Codes
- **pending** — Order placed but not yet processed.
- **processing** — Payment confirmed, awaiting shipment.
- **shipped** — Dispatched to carrier.
- **delivered** — Confirmed delivery.
- **cancelled** — Order voided; excluded from revenue totals.
- **returned** — Goods sent back; excluded from revenue totals.

## Revenue & Targets
- **Gross Revenue**: Sum of `total_amount` across all delivered and shipped orders.
- **Net Revenue**: Gross Revenue minus returned order values.
- **Sales Target**: Monthly target stored in the `sales_targets` table, per category.
- **Attainment %**: (Actual Sales / Sales Target) × 100.

## Date Conventions
- Fiscal year follows the calendar year (Jan–Dec).
- All dates stored as TEXT in ISO-8601 format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Use `strftime('%Y-%m', order_date)` for monthly grouping in SQLite.
- Use `strftime('%Y', order_date)` for annual grouping.
